Black belts, competitors, referees, and mentors — every coach here believes jiu jitsu builds character first. Many started in social projects themselves, and now they teach because someone once made room for them on the mat.

Founder / Instructor

Ron Mukai

ron [at] elajj.com

Black BeltFounder

Ron started jiu jitsu at the age of 39, earned his black belt in 2017, and is a founder or partner in several schools — Ethos Jiu Jitsu in Fullerton (2016), East LA Jiu Jitsu (2022), 2002 Jiu Jitsu in Boston (2024), and Boyle Heights Jiu Jitsu (2025). A recovering lawyer, broker, and real estate developer, his real passion is building character through jiu jitsu.

Ron Mukai is 55 years old and still alive, by the grace of God. He’s also married to his awesome wife Wendy and has 4 awesome kids and 1 awesome daughter-in-law, also by the grace of God.

Ron continues to train daily, as well as teach classes, mentor youth, run his real estate businesses and lead the non-profit (and occasionally sleep). For Ron, the process of jiu jitsu completely changed every aspect of his life, his health, and how he faced personal hardship. This perspective allows him to teach smaller, weaker, slower individuals to overcome their physical (and mental) shortcomings and use the techniques of jiu jitsu to control much bigger, stronger and faster opponents.

Ron has deep roots in East LA. His grandparents moved to East LA in the 1920s. Ron’s parents were both born in East LA and met at Garfield High. Ron was born at White Memorial hospital and has been intimately involved in East LA community and business affairs since 1995. He is a practicing attorney, broker and commercial real estate developer in East LA. In 2003 he developed and still owns and manages East LA Civic Center Plaza on the corner of 3rd Street and Mednik Ave.

Ron sees many parallels between his Christian faith and the principles of jiu jitsu including faith, humility, perseverance, courage, loyalty and self control. He has been married to his beautiful wife, Wendy (also a black belt) for the last 25 years and shares the mat with his 4 awesome kids: Diego (20, brown belt), Xanti (17, blue belt), Mila (12, grey belt) and Eze (8, grey belt), and daughter-in-law Emily (white belt with 1 stripe!).

Ron occasionally competes and won a gold medal as a black belt at the 2023 IBJJF Orange County Open, as well as a silver medal at the IBJJF Pans as a brown belt.

Ron also serves on the Board of Directors for Movements of Grace, a non-profit serving impoverished communities in Colombia and Venezuela, and on the Board of his church, Calvary La Habra.

Ron’s motto is “today is the best day of my life,” so today is going to be the best day of his life!

Founder / Instructor

Scott Torres

Black BeltFounder

Scott began training Brazilian Jiu Jitsu in 2010 upon his discharge from active service in the United States Navy. A lifelong wrestler, he was awarded his black belt by Professor Johnny Ramirez in July of 2019.

Scott wanted something more than what traditional gyms had to offer, and began his jiu jitsu training at Newbreed Mixed Martial Arts under the guidance of Professor Johnny Ramirez.

In 2012, Scott showed interest in teaching and began as an assistant instructor in various kids classes at Newbreed. Over the years he was given additional opportunities to instruct adult classes at the beginner level. By 2018, Scott became one of many instructors responsible for teaching adult jiu jitsu at all levels, and alongside Professor Ramirez he instructed and coached the Newbreed competition team.

Scott is a Christian, and has found commonality between the road to black belt and his walk with the Lord.

Scott is a professional historian. Over the 9 years he trained full time, he completed both undergraduate and graduate degrees from CSU Fullerton. He currently works as an Architectural Historian in the city of Pasadena.

Scott’s love for jiu jitsu and history has led him to various states and countries. He studied history and trained jiu jitsu while living in Berlin, Germany for a summer — a stay that led to many great friendships and invitations to train all over the city.

Scott has been married to his wife Jessica for three years. While not training, Scott and Jessica enjoy spending time at the beach and taking long walks with their German Shepherd, Sunny.

Head Instructor

Rodnei (Roy) Barbosa

2nd Degree Black BeltCompetition

Rodnei is a 2nd degree black belt in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, training since 2004 and a black belt since 2015. He started in a social project in Formiga, Brazil under Professor Lourenço Nascimento and began competing at 10 years old.

Born in the small city of Formiga, Minas Gerais, Rodnei fell in love with the art early and went on to win many national and international tournaments including the Brazilian Nationals, South American, European, World Pro and IBJJF No-Gi Worlds.

In 2019 he moved to Doha, Qatar, where he worked as a jiu jitsu and self defense coach for the National Service in Qatar.

Rodnei also has extensive experience teaching jiu jitsu to kids and adults, dating back to 2013. He moved to the United States in March of 2023 with his wife, Karen, and currently teaches at Ethos Jiu Jitsu Fullerton and East LA Jiu Jitsu.

Instructor

Filipe Pimentel

2nd Degree Black BeltIBJJF Referee

Filipe is a second degree black belt in Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, training since 2009 and starting at age 14 in a social project in a poor community in Brazil. He is also an international referee who has officiated the world’s biggest tournaments.

Filipe started his competitive career in 2011, winning at the state, national and international level. In 2013 he began his refereeing career and has since refereed internationally at the largest tournaments in the world, including the Worlds, Pans, European, South American, Brazilian and American Nationals from the IBJJF/CBJJ/USBJJ federations, and World Pro, Grand Slams and Nationals from the AJP/ADWPJJC federations, among many others.

He has also been teaching since 2015 at both regular academies and social projects. Besides jiu jitsu, Filipe has trained and competed in American football, basketball, judo, CrossFit, tennis and capoeira.

Filipe is also a civil engineer, graduating from university in 2018 and actively licensed in Brazil since 2020. He currently teaches at Ethos Jiu Jitsu Fullerton and East LA Jiu Jitsu.

Instructor

Jerardo (Jerry) Linares

2nd Degree Black BeltEthos Founder

Jerardo is a second degree black belt, one of the founders of Ethos Jiu-Jitsu, and has been training for over 20 years. He teaches a morning class at East LA Jiu Jitsu and visits night classes to train with the students.

Jerardo first got introduced to jiu-jitsu while attending San Diego State University, joining the local college jiu-jitsu club while studying Business Finance. Before starting jiu-jitsu he played soccer for many years, and still enjoys kicking the ball around.

Jerardo is passionate about coaching his students to be better human beings through jiu-jitsu, and is an active competitor.

When not teaching he works as a Real Estate Broker for Millenia Real Estate, a local company he founded. When not working he enjoys spending time with his wife Kimberly and their two dogs, Dexter and Rio. They enjoy riding bikes along the LA River, camping and hiking.

Instructor

Wendy Mukai

Black BeltKids & Adults

Wendy received her black belt on the podium of the IBJJF Master Worlds in the summer of 2023, after decisively winning her division as a brown belt. She helps teach both the kids and adult classes and continues to train daily.

In addition to her coaching role at East LA Jiu Jitsu, Wendy and her husband Ron are part owners of Ethos Jiu Jitsu in Fullerton, CA. Wendy also competes and has won gold as both a blue belt and a brown belt at the IBJJF Master Worlds, the most prestigious tournament in the world for competitors over 30.

Wendy’s path to jiu jitsu happened later in life, alongside her husband, when they signed up their then 5-year-old son in 2009 — and the process completely changed every aspect of her life. She spent 7 years off the mat raising a family of fighters. One by one, all her children trained and gave her a greater desire to one day practice the art herself. After her fourth and final child was born, she was able to train alongside her family.

Jiu jitsu changed her perspective about being a smaller, weaker, slower individual with health problems. She had to overcome her physical shortcomings and use technique to control much bigger, stronger and faster opponents. She sees jiu jitsu as a passion to express herself artistically, spiritually and relationally, and loves how its principles apply to their Christian faith and everyday life.

Wendy has deep roots in Latin culture as a first generation Mexican. She was born in Gómez Palacio, Durango, Mexico and came to the United States at the age of 3. Raised in Orange County, she was the first grandchild in her family to graduate from college. Wendy graduated from FIDM (Fashion Institute of Design and Merchandising) and had a 10 year career as a fashion designer.

She and Ron have 4 kids: Diego, Xanti, Mila and Eze. Her entire family trains jiu jitsu together.

Leo Vilela

Black BeltCompetition

Leonardo de Barros Vilela (Léo) is a black belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, earning his black belt in June 2023. He began training at age 6 in a social project in Poço Fundo, Brazil, and has been passionate about giving back to his community ever since.

Léo moved to a larger city to train with Professor Paulo Cezar Rezende, where he competed successfully in the Brazilian Nationals and South American Championships.

His international achievements include standout performances at the European IBJJF (2020, 2022), AJP Grand Slam London (2022), and World Pro (2021). Léo is recognized for his skill and dedication, both on and off the mat.
